1. Introduction

Welcome to Beyond The Game (BTG), a comprehensive platform designed to help poker players improve their game through disciplined habit tracking, session logging, educational content, and community engagement.

BTG combines personal development tools with poker-specific analytics to give you a complete picture of your journey as a player. Whether you're tracking daily study habits, logging your live sessions, or following other players' progress, BTG provides the tools you need to stay accountable and improve.

Key Features at a Glance

  • Goal & Habit Tracking – Create and track daily, weekly, and monthly habits
  • Poker Session Logging – Record all your poker sessions with detailed analytics
  • Educational Content – Access lectures, videos, and questionnaires
  • Leaderboards – Compete with other players and track your ranking
  • Railbirds – Follow other players and support their journey

2. Getting Started

Onboarding Process

When you first join BTG, you'll be guided through an onboarding process designed to help you get the most out of the platform. The onboarding includes:

  1. Welcome & Introduction – Learn about BTG's core features and philosophy
  2. Profile Setup – Configure your display name, photo, and team assignment
  3. Initial Goals – Select from recommended habits or create your own
  4. Preferences – Set your preferred currency, time format, and other settings
  5. Onboarding Lectures – Complete introductory educational content
Complete the onboarding process to unlock all features. Some content may be restricted until onboarding is finished.

Navigating the App

BTG uses a bottom navigation bar (on mobile) or sidebar (on desktop) to access the main sections:

Overview Analytics dashboard with monthly summaries
Poker Session logging and poker statistics
Learn Educational content, lectures, and resources
Leaderboard Rankings and competitive standings

3. Goals & Habit Tracking

The heart of BTG is its habit tracking system. By consistently tracking your daily activities, you build the discipline necessary for long-term poker success.

Creating Goals

To create a new goal:

  1. Navigate to the Home screen
  2. Tap the + Add Goal button
  3. Choose a goal type (Yes/No, Quantitative, or Habit)
  4. Fill in the goal details: name, description, category, and icon
  5. Configure the frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
  6. Set point values and any additional options
  7. Save your goal

Goal Types

Yes/No Goals

Simple binary goals that you either complete or don't complete each day. Examples include "Did I meditate today?" or "Did I review my hands?"

Quantitative Goals

Goals that track numeric values with customizable units. Perfect for tracking hours studied, pages read, or hands played. You can set:

  • Target Value – The number you aim to reach
  • Unit – What you're measuring (hours, pages, hands, etc.)
  • Step Increment – How much to add/subtract with each tap

Recurring Habits

Habits that accumulate over a period. For example, "Exercise 5 times per week" or "Study 20 hours per month." These support:

  • Weekly Accumulation – Track progress toward a weekly target
  • Monthly Accumulation – Track progress toward a monthly target
  • Custom Active Days – Specify which days of the week the habit applies

Tracking Your Habits

Your daily habits appear on the Home screen. To track completion:

  • Yes/No Goals: Tap the goal card to toggle completion (shows swipe animation)
  • Quantitative Goals: Tap to open the input dialog
  • Time-based Goals: Enter time in hours:minutes format

Quantitative Input Dialog

When logging quantitative goals, a dialog appears with:

  • Minus button (-) – Decrease value by step amount
  • Plus button (+) – Increase value by step amount
  • Value display – Click to edit the number directly
  • Progress bar – Shows current progress vs. weekly/monthly target
  • Log button – Save the tracked value
You can reorganize your goals by dragging and dropping them into your preferred order.

Streaks & Progress

BTG tracks your consistency through streaks. A streak counts how many consecutive days, weeks, or months you've completed a goal. Streaks are displayed on each goal card and in the overview.

Visual indicators help you see your progress at a glance:

  • Dots – Show completion status for each day of the current week
  • Progress Bars – Display progress toward weekly/monthly targets
  • Streak Counter – Shows your current consecutive completion streak

4. Poker Session Tracking

Keep detailed records of every poker session to analyze your performance and identify trends over time.

Logging Sessions

To log a new poker session:

  1. On the Home screen, find the Poker Sessions section
  2. Click the "Log Session" button (plus icon)
  3. Select game type: Cash Game, Tournament, or Rakeback
  4. Choose game variants (NLH, PLO, etc.) from the autocomplete dropdown
  5. Enter session details based on game type
  6. Optionally rate your performance (Focus, Power, Discipline)
  7. Add notes
  8. Click "Log Session" to save
Click any existing session card to edit it. Use the "Delete Session" button (red) to remove a session.

Session Details

Each session can include the following information:

Field Description
Date & Time When the session took place
Game Type NLH, PLO, PLO5c, PLO6c, Short Deck, HORSE, etc.
Stakes/Big Blind The blind level played
Buy-in Total amount bought in for
Cash-out Total amount cashed out
Hours Played Session duration
Currency Currency used for the session
Promotions/Rakeback Any additional earnings from promotions
Performance Ratings Self-assessment of Focus, Power, and Discipline (1-5)
Notes Free-form notes about the session

Poker Analytics

BTG automatically calculates key statistics from your logged sessions:

  • Total P&L – Your overall profit or loss
  • Hourly Rate – Average earnings per hour
  • Session Count – Number of sessions played
  • Hours Played – Total time at the tables
  • Win Rate by Game Type – Performance breakdown by variant

View your statistics on a monthly basis or filter by date range, game type, and stakes.

5. Poker Events & Series

Track your participation in poker tournament series and special events.

Series Hub

The Series Hub displays all poker series. Each series card shows:

  • Status badge – "Joined", "Not Interested", or "Available"
  • Series name and date range
  • Location (online or physical address)

Click any series card to view its details and events.

Joining a Series

  1. Open a series from the Series Hub
  2. Click the user icon button in the top right (color indicates current status)
  3. Select your participation level: "All events", "Some dates", "Cash only", or "Not interested"
  4. Click "Save"

Event Details

Each series contains multiple events. Events display:

  • Event name, date, and time
  • Game type (Cash or Tournament)
  • Stakes or buy-in amount
  • Your participation status and results

Tracking Event Participation

For each event, you can set your status:

  • Interested – Planning to play
  • Playing – Currently participating
  • Done Playing – Finished the event
  • Not Interested – Skipping this event

Logging Poker Sessions for Events

Sessions logged while in an event are automatically linked to that event, allowing you to track buy-ins, cashes, and results per event.

Admin: Creating Series & Events

Administrators can create new series using the "Add Series" button, specifying name, dates, and location. Events can be added within each series.

6. Analytics & Dashboards

Overview Dashboard

The Overview screen provides a comprehensive view of your progress:

  • Monthly View – See your habit completion rate for the current month
  • Daily Breakdown – Tap any day to see detailed completion data
  • Weekly Summaries – Track week-over-week progress
  • Poker Stats – Monthly P&L, hours, and session summaries
  • Insights – Trends and patterns in your data

Leaderboards

Compare your team's performance with others across the BTG community. The Leaderboard page is accessible from the sidebar and ranks teams by their average points per member.

Leaderboard Tabs

The leaderboard offers two tabs to switch between ranking types:

  • Tracking – Teams ranked by average habit tracking points per member
  • Poker – Teams ranked by poker performance, with sub-tabs for:
    • Profit (BB) – Average big blind profit
    • Volume – Total poker volume

Month Navigation

Use the left and right arrow buttons to browse leaderboard data for different months. Historical data is available going back to October 2025.

Podium & Rankings Display

The top 3 teams are displayed in a visual podium with gold, silver, and bronze styling. Each podium entry shows the team initials, name, and key metric. If your team is ranked #1, a confetti animation celebrates the achievement.

Below the podium, the Full Rankings table lists all teams with:

  • Rank – Position with color-coded border (gold, silver, bronze for top 3)
  • Team – Team avatar and name
  • Sessions – Number of sessions for the month
  • Points / Metric – Average points, BB profit, or volume depending on the selected tab
Your team's row is highlighted so you can quickly spot your ranking.

7. Educational Content

BTG provides a rich library of educational content to support your poker development.

Lectures & Videos

Access a curated collection of video lectures and educational materials organized into tabs:

  • New & Noteworthy – Recently added content
  • Onboarding – Essential content for new members
  • Main Calls – Archive of live coaching calls
  • Study Panels – Deep-dive study sessions
  • Homework – Practice assignments

Each content card shows: thumbnail, title, duration (clock icon), comment count, and star rating. A green checkmark badge indicates completed content.

Video Player Features

  • Play/Pause – Standard video controls
  • Progress bar – Seekable timeline with progress tracking
  • Playback speed – Options from 0.5x to 2x speed
  • Full-screen mode – Immersive viewing
  • Volume control – Adjust audio level
  • Chat overlay – Some videos include synced live chat replay

Automatic Progress Tracking

BTG automatically tracks your video progress including:

  • Percentage watched and milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)
  • Engaged viewing time (only counts while actively watching)
  • Pause and seek events
  • Resume position for returning later

Rating Content

Rate videos and content using the 5-star rating system. You can rate in 0.5 star increments using either star buttons or a slider control.

Questionnaires

Interactive questionnaires test your understanding and reinforce learning.

Completing Questionnaires

  1. Open a questionnaire from the Learn section
  2. Progress through questions (progress bar shows Q1, Q2, Q3...)
  3. Answer using: multiple choice, checkboxes, or text input (depending on question type)
  4. Questions marked as required must be answered before submitting
  5. Click "Submit" to save your answers
Your questionnaire responses are saved and can be viewed by admins for feedback.

8. Railbirds (Social Features)

The Railbirds feature lets you follow other players and build a supportive community.

Following Players

Add other BTG members as railbirds to follow their progress:

  1. Navigate to the Railbirds section
  2. Click the "Add Railbird" button
  3. Select a member from the dropdown (organized by "My Team" and "Rest of BTG")
  4. Click "Next"
  5. Choose access level: "From this moment forward" (new data only) or "Include past data" (historical access)
  6. Click "Add Railbird" to confirm

Removing Railbirds

To stop following someone, click the X icon on their card in the "My Rail" section, then confirm by clicking "Yes".

Player Overview

Click on any railbird card to view their detailed Overview page showing:

  • Habit completion rates and streaks
  • Poker session statistics
  • Monthly progress data
  • Recent activity

Support & Encouragement

When viewing a player's overview, use the floating action buttons in the bottom right corner:

Button Color Purpose
🎉 Cheer Green Send encouragement for good performance
💙 Comfort Blue Offer support during tough times

Each button opens a dialog where you can write a personal message before sending.

People Watching Me

The "People Watching Me" section shows who has added you as a railbird. Build accountability by knowing others are following your journey.

10. Settings & Preferences

Customize BTG to match your preferences.

Display Settings

Setting Options

Theme Light or Dark mode
First Day of Week Sunday or Monday

Poker Settings

Setting Description
Default Currency Currency for new poker sessions
Game Variants Your preferred game types for quick selection

Notification Settings

Control which notifications you receive and how they're delivered.

Personal Access Tokens (API Keys)

For advanced users and developers, BTG supports Personal Access Tokens for API access. Access this from Settings > API Keys.

Creating a Token

  1. Click the "New Token" button (key icon)
  2. Enter a name for your token (e.g., "CLI tool")
  3. Click "Create"
  4. Important: Copy your token immediately! It will only be shown once.
  5. Use the copy icon button to copy the token to your clipboard
Store your token securely. If you lose it, you'll need to create a new one.

Token Scopes

Currently supported scope: pokersessions.readwrite – allows reading and writing poker session data via the API.

Managing Tokens

  • View all your tokens with creation date and last used date
  • Click the trash icon to revoke a token (immediate, no confirmation)

11. Admin Features

Administrators have access to additional features for managing the BTG platform.

These features are only available to users with administrator privileges.

Admin Navigation

Admin pages are accessible from the sidebar menu:

  • Users – User management
  • Teams – Team management
  • Content – Lecture/video management
  • Deals – Promotions management
  • Poker Analytics – Player statistics
  • Engagement – User engagement tracking
  • Trackings – Habit tracking reports
  • Email Templates – Email template management
  • Default Goals – Default habit templates
  • Advanced Tools – Leaderboard calculation, Discord upvotes, and utilities

User Management

Users are organized by team with the following action buttons on each user card:

Button Icon Action
Email Envelope Opens email dialog to send email and view email history
Push Bell Opens push notification dialog
Edit Pen Opens user edit dialog
Delete X mark Deletes user (with confirmation)

Viewing Email History

To see all emails sent to a specific user:

  1. Go to Admin > Users
  2. Find the user and click the envelope icon (Email button)
  3. In the Email Dialog, click the mail history icon (multiple envelopes) in the top right corner
  4. View all sent emails as expandable accordions showing: recipient, subject, date sent, and full email content

Impersonating Users

To view the app as another user for troubleshooting:

  1. Go to Admin > Users
  2. Click the pen icon (Edit) on the user
  3. In the Edit User dialog, click the user-secret icon button
  4. The page reloads and you'll see a yellow warning banner showing "Impersonating: [name]"
  5. Click "Cancel Impersonation" button to return to your account

Viewing Which Lectures Users Watched

There are multiple ways to see user lecture/video completion:

Method 1: Engagement Dashboard

  1. Go to Admin > Engagement
  2. Filter by time period, team, or search for specific users
  3. Click on any user row to expand their details
  4. View the VIDEOS section showing checkmarks (watched), percentages (partial), or circles (not watched)

Method 2: Content Results Button

  1. Go to Admin > Content
  2. Click the "Results" button (eye icon) in the top right
  3. A fullscreen dialog shows all users and all content in a table
  4. Filter by specific member or specific content item
  5. Green = completed, Yellow = partial, Checkmark = watched, Percentage = progress

Method 3: Eye Icon on Individual Content

  1. Go to Admin > Content
  2. Click the eye icon on any video or content item
  3. See "Seen by X people" with list of users and their progress percentages
  4. Use "Send Reminder" button to remind users who haven't completed

Poker Analytics

The Poker Analytics page provides comprehensive session data with filters:

  • Game Type – All, Cash, or MTT
  • Tracking – Hours or Hands
  • Stakes/Variants – Multi-select filters
  • Date Range – From/To date pickers
  • Team/Players – Filter by team or specific players

Features include: summary cards, player distribution bar, expandable player leaderboard, downswing watch, session quality analysis, and results by variant.

Engagement Analytics

Track user engagement across multiple metrics:

  • Calls – Live session attendance or recording views
  • Homework – Assignment submissions
  • Discord – Discord activity
  • Tracking – Habit tracking activity

The "At Risk" section highlights users with no activity in 7+ days.

Team Management

  • Create and edit teams with logos
  • Each team card has Edit (pen) and Delete (X) buttons
  • "Add Team" button creates new teams

Content Management

  • Hierarchical folder structure with drag-and-drop reordering
  • Create videos, questionnaires, and HTML content
  • Set reminders for incomplete content
  • Download questionnaire responses as CSV

Email Templates

  • Create reusable email templates
  • Rich text HTML editor
  • Use templates when sending emails to users

Advanced Tools

The Advanced Tools page provides administrative utilities for calculating leaderboards, managing Discord upvote points, and other maintenance tasks. Access it from the sidebar under Admin > Advanced Tools.

Calculate Leaderboard

Generate the monthly leaderboard rankings based on user tracking data:

  1. Enter the Month (1–12) and Year
  2. Click "Calculate Leaderboard"
  3. On success, a summary displays the total number of users and total points distributed
  4. Click "Download Excel (Daily Points)" to export a CSV with a day-by-day breakdown of points per user, including tracking, Discord, day off, weekly, monthly, and every-day bonus categories
Leaderboard calculation must be run before the Leaderboard page displays data for that month.

Calculate Discord Upvotes

The upvote system captures emoji reactions from the BTG Discord community channel and converts them into reward points for members:

  1. Enter the Month (1–12) and Year
  2. Click "Calculate Upvotes" to fetch and process Discord reaction data
  3. Review the Points Distribution table showing each member's upvote count and calculated points
  4. Click "Download Full Sheet" to export a CSV of the full voting matrix (who upvoted whom)
  5. Click "Commit Points" to save the point allocation to user accounts

Upvote Points Distribution

After calculation, the points distribution table displays:

  • Member – The team member who received upvotes
  • Upvotes Received – Total number of emoji reactions received
  • Points – Calculated point value based on the upvote count

Members are sorted by points in descending order. Only members with points greater than zero are shown.

12. Data Management

Importing Data

Import tracking data from CSV files to migrate from other systems or restore backups:

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Select Import Data
  3. Choose the data type to import
  4. Select your CSV file
  5. Map columns to BTG fields
  6. Confirm the import

Exporting Data

Export your data for backup or analysis:

  • Habit tracking history
  • Poker session records
  • Goal configurations

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data through the Settings menu. This action is irreversible, so please ensure you have exported any data you wish to keep.

Contact an administrator if you need assistance with data management.
